## Lessons
- 2026-10-14 (prior): A single poll should move ORACLE's current-events estimate by 5 points at most. Polling averages and several high-quality polls matter more.
- 2026-10-14 (prior): Betting markets are strong benchmarks. If ORACLE differs from a busy market by more than 15 points, the briefing must say exactly why.
- 2026-10-14 (prior): Ceasefire and peace-deal announcements are often premature. Don't treat one as lasting until it actually holds.
- 2026-10-14 (prior): A Senate poll lead under about 4 points is close to a toss-up. In Maine in 2020, Collins beat her polls by a wide margin.
- 2026-10-14: Stability bands recalibrated against 20 historical eras measured with the same formula. Calm eras (1851, 1956, 1997) sit below 0.40. Tense eras that held (Watergate, 9/11, 1979, Jan 6, 1968) sit at 0.40-0.60. Severe crises that bent the system (2008, 2020, 1933, Weimar 1923, the 1991 Soviet coup) sit at 0.60-0.75. Civil wars (49 BC, 1861) sit above 0.75, with the Bastille (0.746) right at the line. Bands are now 0.40 / 0.60 / 0.75, adding Path Omega (collapse).
- 2026-10-16: In an energy shock, producer prices react more than consumer prices: September gasoline was +9.1% in CPI but +20.3% in PPI, and PPI final demand jumped 1.3% (ORACLE gave a 0.5%+ rise only 48%; history-based CLIO said 35%). When CPI energy jumps 5%+, treat a 0.5%+ PPI headline as likely (65-75%), not unusual.
- 2026-11-04: 2026-11-04: In a wave year, ORACLE's Senate/governor calls of 40-60% on Democrats all broke the same way (TX, IA, OH, KS, SC flipped). Race forecasts are highly correlated; treat 'toss-up' seats as one shared bet on the national environment rather than independent coin flips.
